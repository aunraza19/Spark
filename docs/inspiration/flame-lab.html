The challenge in three lines

Input
Real microgravity combustion records from NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics archive, plus technical reports and project records.
Task
Build AI-assisted insight that helps people understand how fire ignites, spreads and goes out in space.
Why
Spacecraft materials and cabin conditions on future missions must be judged for fire risk, and gravity changes how flames behave.

The physics in one screen

On Earth, hot gas rises and pulls fresh oxygen in, so flames are tall and teardrop-shaped. In freefall that buoyant flow vanishes. Oxygen reaches the flame by diffusion and by cabin ventilation, so flames are rounder, dimmer and can persist in conditions that would put them out on the ground. Try it in the flame lab.

Two-stage burning reported for NASA FLEX droplet experiments

1. Hot flameNormal burning of a fuel droplet.
2. Radiative extinctionHeat loss by radiation puts the visible flame out.
3. Cool flameLow-temperature chemistry keeps burning with no visible flame.

Variable importance

Gravity is given in g. Moon surface is about 0.17 g. ISS is about 0.00001 g. Pressure is in kPa; sea-level air is 101.3 kPa. Airflow is limited to 0.1 to 5 cm/s, the range tested in FLEX-2. The fuel list and tested ranges come from the FLEX-2 experimental table (PSI-68, DOI 10.60555/byp8-h018). Fuel offsets, the extinction chance, the uncertainty band and the importance bars all come from placeholder rules, not NASA measurements; replace them with a model fitted to the PSI results before presenting anything.

Size of each source

Sizes are as downloaded by the community mirror. The full PSI collection is about 13.5 TB, mostly video and raw images.

Known data problems

  • About 2,600 legacy PSI files return 404 on NASA's server. About 1,600 have no backup, mostly from FLEX-2.
  • About 23,000 SLICE image-matrix files (about 7 GB) were skipped by that download.
  • MAPTIS, the materials flammability database, has restricted access.
  • Official statement and datasets were released 28 Oct 2026.

Plan around these gaps and say so in your pitch. Honest limits score better than a model that hides them.
